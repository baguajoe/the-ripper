## The Ripper 1.2.0

The chopper becomes a full sampling workstation: trim, shape and play your pads, program beats
with a step sequencer and a MIDI controller, add effects, resample, fit samples to your tempo and
key, save sessions, and drag the whole beat — or one track per pad — into your DAW.

Everything you hear goes through one new pad engine, and every export is rendered by that same
engine, so what you play is exactly what lands in the DAW.

### Trim
- **Double-click a pad, a slice row or a slice on the waveform** to open the trim view: a zoomed
  waveform of that slice with draggable start / end markers.
- **Nudge** with ← / → (1 ms), Shift (10 ms), Ctrl (1 beat), or **type exact times**.
- **Snap** to zero crossings (on by default — no clicks), the nearest transient, or the grid.
- **Loop audition** replays the slice from the start on every change. **Link neighbors** keeps
  slices contiguous (off by default). Edges dragged on the main waveform snap the same way.

### Pad sound shaping (Pad tab)
- **Tune** ±24 semitones + fine ±50 cents (changes speed, like a classic sampler), or **Keep
  length** to time-stretch instead.
- **Envelope** (attack, decay, sustain, release) with a small picture of its shape.
- **Filter**: low-pass / high-pass / band-pass, cutoff + resonance.
- **Velocity → volume** and **velocity → filter**, and **pan**.
- **Copy / paste / swap pads** (Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V; Alt-drag a pad onto another), **rename and
  color** from the pad's right-click menu.

### Step sequencer (Sequencer tab)
- One row per pad of the current bank, **16 / 32 / 64 steps**; click steps on/off, drag a step up
  or down for velocity, accent row, **swing 50–75 %**.
- **Patterns A–H** with copy / paste / clear, chained into a **song**.
- Play / Stop / Loop, **count-in**, **metronome**. **Live record** from the keys, the mouse or a
  MIDI controller with **quantize** (off, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32) and **overdub**; one take = one undo.
- **Note repeat**: hold a pad at 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/8T or 1/16T.
- Mono / poly, choke and every pad setting apply. Timing is sample-exact.

### MIDI controllers
- Any MIDI pad controller or keyboard, velocity included. Notes 36–51 play pads 1–16.
- **MIDI learn** for pads and Play / Stop / Record — it's remembered.

### Vintage, effects, resample
- **Vintage 12-bit** (12-bit, ~40 kHz with anti-alias filtering) or a warmer **Vintage 16-bit**,
  per bank.
- **Effects** per pad (drive, tempo-synced delay, reverb) and on the **master bus** (+ a
  compressor), wet/dry on each, rendered into every export.
- **Resample** a pass of the pattern or song — or a live take — into a new track, ready to chop.

### Fit the sample to your beat
- **Match** (editor header): time-stretch to a project BPM without changing the pitch, and/or
  shift to a project key without changing the tempo. You get a new track with your chops, pads
  and patterns carried over. Uses Signalsmith Stretch (MIT).

### Export the beat
- **Beat WAV** and **MIDI** (one note per pad from C1, velocity + swing applied).
- **Pad stems**: one WAV per used pad, all from bar 1 and the same length — one DAW track per pad,
  already in time. Options: include effects, include the master bus.
- **Chops in place**: one WAV per chop, each with only that chop at its original position,
  silence elsewhere, all the same length (selection or whole song) — line them up at bar 1 and
  every chop sits where it was.
- Numbered file names (`…_pad01_Kick.wav`, `…_slice01_….wav`) so DAWs import them in order.
- Save, export to a folder, or **drag several files in one go** into the DAW. The Kit zip now
  also holds your patterns as MIDI, the beat, the pad stems and the chops in place.

### Sessions, audio, polish
- **Save / Save as / Open** a `.ripper` session (Ctrl+S / Ctrl+Shift+S / Ctrl+O) with the audio,
  chops, trims, pads, patterns, effects and settings. **Recent sessions** on the Library page.
  **Autosave every minute.** Saved sessions never expire; older session files always open.
- **Audio** settings: output device (switches instantly), buffer size, the reported latency and
  **Measure pad latency**.
- Press **?** for every keyboard shortcut. Tooltips on every control. A short, skippable
  first-run tour.

### Free vs Pro
Pro: patterns C–H, resample, effects, vintage mode, time-stretch / key-shift, batch and kit
export. Free: import, analysis, chopping, pads + shaping, trim, patterns A–B, single-file exports,
Fast stems. HQ (AI) stems stay free for now.

### Known limits
- **Latency on Windows**: pads play through the system's shared audio output. Use *Audio →
  Measure pad latency* and tell us the number on your PC; a native low-latency output (WASAPI
  exclusive / CoreAudio) is planned if it's above ~10 ms. ASIO isn't included (licensing, see
  LICENSES.md).
- Session files open from inside the app (Open… / recent list); double-clicking a `.ripper` file
  in Explorer / Finder doesn't open it yet.
