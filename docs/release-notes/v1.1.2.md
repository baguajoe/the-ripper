## The Ripper 1.1.2

### Fixed
- **The editor no longer jumps around while playing.** The editor now fits the window, so there's no page scrolling or scrollbars at 1280×800 and larger. The waveform and pads resize with the window, and the slice list scrolls inside its own panel.
- **Nothing scrolls on its own.** The text that changes during playback (Play/Pause, the time display) has fixed widths, so nothing shifts as it updates. The waveform also no longer scrolls to follow the playhead by default: it opens zoomed to fit the whole track. If you zoom in and want it to follow, tick **Follow playhead**.
- **Uninstalling removes your library (Windows).** Uninstalling now deletes the app's track library, settings and logs, so a reinstall starts empty. Installing an update over the top still keeps everything. Files you exported are never touched.
