## The Ripper 1.1.3

Fixes and features from testing 1.1.2 on Windows.

### Chop a selection, not the whole song
- **Select a region** by dragging on the waveform (it snaps to the grid), by clicking a bar number and shift-clicking another, or with **From playhead: 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 bars**. The start and end bars are shown. **All** selects the whole track.
- **Chop only chops the selection.** With no selection it takes 4 bars from the playhead. Modes: **Even 8 / 16 / 32**, **Grid** (1/4 beat … 1 bar), **Transients** (at most 16 slices by default, the strongest hits, with a sensitivity slider), **Manual**.
- **Changing the mode or size re-chops right away**, and the slice count updates immediately (this was the bug where 1 bar → 4 bars didn't change anything).
- **New chops go into the next empty pad bank** (A–H), or tick **Replace current bank**.
- **No tiny slices:** nothing is shorter than 50 ms, and a gap of less than half a beat before bar 1 merges into the first slice (no more 0.1 s slice01).
- **Undo / redo** for chops, slice edits and banks: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z (Cmd on a Mac), or the buttons.

### Sampler-style pads
- **Mono (chops cut each other)**, on by default per bank: hitting a pad stops the one before it in that bank (5 ms fade, no click). Switch a bank to **Poly** for one-shots that should ring out.
- Per pad: **One-shot** or **Note-on** (plays while held). **Choke all / Unchoke all** per bank.
- **Esc** or the **Stop** button stops all playback.

### HQ (AI) stems: a one-click, free add-on
- **Get HQ (AI) stems** downloads the AI engine for your computer: an NVIDIA GPU build on Windows PCs with an NVIDIA graphics card (RTX 20 series and newer), an Apple Silicon build on Macs, a CPU build otherwise. You see the size first, then a progress bar. A failed download resumes where it stopped, every file is checksum-verified, and **Remove** deletes it all again. HQ (AI) appears straight away, no restart.
- Models: **4 stems** (drums, bass, vocals, other), **6 stems** (+ guitar, piano) and **2 stems** (vocals / instrumental). The estimated processing time is shown before you separate, and it gets more accurate after the first run.
- HQ (AI) stems are **free** for now.
- The model weights are downloaded from the Demucs author's own repositories. Their licence is unclear; see LICENSES.md.

### Fast stems say what they can do
- **Fast (no AI)** now makes **Drums / Bass / Music** (it no longer pretends to find vocals, which ended up in the wrong stem). For vocals, use HQ (AI).
- New Fast option: **Instrumental (center cancel)**, a mid/side vocal remover.
- In the mixer, **click a stem's name to hear it on its own**.
